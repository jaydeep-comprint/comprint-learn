# Acceleration

**Acceleration** is the rate of change of velocity: how much the velocity changes each second.

a = (v − u) ÷ t, where u is the initial velocity, v the final velocity and t the time taken.

Its unit is metres per second per second, **m/s²**. Acceleration is a vector: it has a direction.

- If the acceleration points the same way as the motion, the object speeds up.
- If it points the opposite way, the object slows down. This is called **retardation** (or deceleration),
  and with the direction of motion taken as positive the acceleration is negative.

For uniform acceleration, rearranging gives the first equation of motion: **v = u + at**.
