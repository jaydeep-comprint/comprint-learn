## Worked example: elimination

Solve $3x + 4y = 10$ and $2x - 2y = 2$.

1. Make the $y$-coefficients opposite: multiply the second equation by 2, giving $4x - 4y = 4$.
2. Add it to the first: $(3x + 4y) + (4x - 4y) = 10 + 4$, so $7x = 14$ and $x = 2$.
3. Substitute into $2x - 2y = 2$: $4 - 2y = 2$, so $y = 1$.
4. Check: $3(2) + 4(1) = 10$ ✓.

If the coefficients of the variable you eliminate have the **same** sign, subtract; if **opposite** signs, add.
