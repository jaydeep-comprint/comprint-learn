## Worked example: average speed over two parts

A cyclist rides 10 km at 10 km/h and then another 10 km at 30 km/h. What is the average speed?

- Time for part 1 = 10 km ÷ 10 km/h = 1 h
- Time for part 2 = 10 km ÷ 30 km/h = ⅓ h
- Total distance = 20 km; total time = 1⅓ h
- Average speed = 20 km ÷ (4/3 h) = **15 km/h**

It is *not* (10 + 30) ÷ 2 = 20 km/h: the cyclist spends longer at the slower speed.
