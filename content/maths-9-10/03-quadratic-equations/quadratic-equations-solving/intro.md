# Solving quadratic equations

A **quadratic equation** in $x$ is an equation that can be written as

$$ax^2 + bx + c = 0, \qquad a \neq 0.$$

This is its **standard form**: everything on the left, 0 on the right, terms in order of power.

- A **root** of the equation is a value of $x$ that makes it true, that is, a zero of the polynomial $ax^2 + bx + c$.
  A quadratic equation has at most two roots.
- **By factorisation**: write $ax^2 + bx + c$ as a product of two linear factors. If a product is 0, one of
  the factors is 0, so each factor gives a root.
- **By the quadratic formula**: when $b^2 - 4ac \ge 0$, the roots are
  $x = \dfrac{-b \pm \sqrt{b^2 - 4ac}}{2a}$. It works even when the factors are hard to find.

In word problems, check every root against the situation: a length, a speed or a number of pens can't be negative.
