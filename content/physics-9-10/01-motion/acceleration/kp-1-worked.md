## Worked example: finding acceleration

A car speeds up from 4 m/s to 16 m/s in 6 s.

a = (v − u) ÷ t = (16 − 4) ÷ 6 = 12 ÷ 6 = **2 m/s²**

The velocity rises by 2 m/s every second.

If speeds are given in km/h, convert first: 36 km/h → 72 km/h in 10 s is 10 m/s → 20 m/s, so
a = (20 − 10) ÷ 10 = 1 m/s².
