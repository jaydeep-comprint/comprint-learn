## Worked example: (x ± y)² and x² − y²

**a)** Expand $(3x + 5)^2$. Use $(x + y)^2 = x^2 + 2xy + y^2$ with $3x$ in place of $x$ and $5$ in place of $y$:
$(3x)^2 + 2 \times 3x \times 5 + 5^2 = 9x^2 + 30x + 25$.
Square the **whole** first term: $(3x)^2 = 9x^2$, not $3x^2$.

**b)** Expand $(2a - 7)^2$. With $(x - y)^2 = x^2 - 2xy + y^2$: $4a^2 - 28a + 49$. Only the middle term is negative.

**c)** Find $98^2$ without long multiplication: $98^2 = (100 - 2)^2 = 10000 - 400 + 4 = 9604$.

**d)** Find $103 \times 97$: $(100 + 3)(100 - 3) = 100^2 - 3^2 = 10000 - 9 = 9991$.

**e)** If $x + y = 7$ and $xy = 12$, then $x^2 + y^2 = (x + y)^2 - 2xy = 49 - 24 = 25$.
