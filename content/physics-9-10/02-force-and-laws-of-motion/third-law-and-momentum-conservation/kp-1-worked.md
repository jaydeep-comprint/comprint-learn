## Worked example: finding the partner force

A swimmer's hands push the water backward with a force of 60 N. What force does the water exert, and on what?

**Step 1: name the two bodies.** The action is "hands push water". The bodies are the *hands* and the *water*.

**Step 2: swap them.** The reaction is "water pushes hands".

**Step 3: same size, opposite direction, same kind.** The water pushes the swimmer's hands **forward** with
**60 N**. Both forces are contact pushes, and both act for exactly as long as the stroke lasts.

Taking forward as positive: the force on the water is −60 N and the force on the hands is **+60 N**.

The same recipe works for any pair:

| Action | Reaction |
|---|---|
| The foot pushes the ground backward | The ground pushes the foot forward |
| The Earth pulls a mango down | The mango pulls the Earth up |
| A rocket pushes hot gases backward | The gases push the rocket forward |

The size of each body doesn't matter. A big magnet pulls a small magnet exactly as hard as the small magnet
pulls the big one.
