## Worked example: solving by drawing

Solve graphically: $x + y = 5$ and $x - y = 1$.

1. Table for $x + y = 5$: $(0, 5)$, $(5, 0)$, $(2, 3)$.
2. Table for $x - y = 1$: $(1, 0)$, $(0, -1)$, $(3, 2)$.
3. Plot both lines on the same axes. They meet at $(3, 2)$.
4. Check in both equations: $3 + 2 = 5$ ✓ and $3 - 2 = 1$ ✓. So $x = 3$, $y = 2$.

A graph is only as accurate as your drawing, so always check the point in both equations.
