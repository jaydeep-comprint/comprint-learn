## Worked example: slowing down (retardation)

A bus moving at 20 m/s brakes and stops in 5 s. Take the direction of motion as positive.

a = (v − u) ÷ t = (0 − 20) ÷ 5 = **−4 m/s²**

The minus sign means the acceleration points *backwards*, against the motion, so the bus slows down. We say
its **retardation** is 4 m/s². (An object moving in the negative direction with a negative acceleration
would be speeding up — the sign is about direction, not "slowing".)
