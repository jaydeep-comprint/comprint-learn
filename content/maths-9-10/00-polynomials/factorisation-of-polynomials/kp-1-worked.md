## Worked example: the factor theorem

**Factor theorem**: $x - a$ is a factor of $p(x)$ if $p(a) = 0$, and if $x - a$ is a factor, then $p(a) = 0$.

**a)** Is $x - 2$ a factor of $p(x) = x^3 - 4x^2 + x + 6$?
$x - 2$ is 0 when $x = 2$, so find $p(2) = 8 - 16 + 2 + 6 = 0$. Yes, $x - 2$ is a factor.

**b)** Is $x + 1$ a factor of $q(x) = x^3 + 2x + 4$?
$x + 1 = x - (-1)$, so find $q(-1) = -1 - 2 + 4 = 1 \ne 0$. No, it is not a factor.
(Don't test $q(1)$ for $x + 1$: the number to try is the one that makes the factor 0.)

**c)** Find $k$ if $x - 3$ is a factor of $r(x) = x^2 + kx - 6$.
$r(3) = 0$: $9 + 3k - 6 = 0$, so $3k = -3$ and $k = -1$.
