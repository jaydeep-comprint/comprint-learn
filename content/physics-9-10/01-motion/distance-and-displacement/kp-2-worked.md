## Worked example: positions on a number line

Take east as the positive direction. A car starts at x = 30 m and drives to x = −20 m.

Displacement = final position − initial position = (−20 m) − (30 m) = **−50 m**.

The minus sign does not mean "less" or "slowing down". It means the displacement points **west** (the
negative direction): the car ends up 50 m west of where it started.
