## Worked example: writing an equation in the form ax + by + c = 0

Write $3x = 2y - 7$ in the form $ax + by + c = 0$ and find $a$, $b$ and $c$.

1. Move every term to the left side, changing the sign of each term you move: $3x - 2y + 7 = 0$.
2. Compare with $ax + by + c = 0$: $a = 3$, $b = -2$, $c = 7$.

Check: putting the terms back, $3x + 7 = 2y$, which is the same as $3x = 2y - 7$. ✓
