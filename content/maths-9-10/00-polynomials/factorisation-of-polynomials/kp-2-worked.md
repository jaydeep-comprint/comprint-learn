## Worked example: splitting the middle term

Factorise $6x^2 + 17x + 5$.

1. Multiply the first and last coefficients: $a \times c = 6 \times 5 = 30$.
2. Find two numbers with **product 30** and **sum 17** (the middle coefficient): $15$ and $2$.
3. Split the middle term with them: $6x^2 + 15x + 2x + 5$.
4. Group in pairs and take out common factors: $3x(2x + 5) + 1(2x + 5)$.
5. Take out the common bracket: $(2x + 5)(3x + 1)$.

Check: $(2x + 5)(3x + 1) = 6x^2 + 2x + 15x + 5 = 6x^2 + 17x + 5$. ✓

**Signs**: for $x^2 - 7x + 12$ the product is $+12$ and the sum $-7$, so both numbers are negative: $-3$ and $-4$,
giving $(x - 3)(x - 4)$. For $x^2 - x - 12$ the product is $-12$, so the numbers have opposite signs: $-4$ and $3$,
giving $(x - 4)(x + 3)$.
