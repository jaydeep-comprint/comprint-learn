## Worked example: rearranging s = v t

For uniform motion, distance s = speed v × time t. To find an unknown, rearrange the equation as in
algebra (do the same thing to both sides):

- Time: divide both sides by v → t = s ÷ v.
  A train at 20 m/s covers 900 m in t = 900 ÷ 20 = **45 s**.
- Speed: divide both sides by t → v = s ÷ t.

If an object starts 30 m from a pole and moves away at 5 m/s, its distance from the pole after t
seconds is **s = 30 + 5t** — a linear equation in two variables, just like in maths.
