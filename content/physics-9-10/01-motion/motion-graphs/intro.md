# Graphs of motion

A graph shows a whole journey at a glance. Time always goes along the horizontal axis.

- A **distance–time graph** shows how far an object has gone at each moment. Its **slope** (steepness) is the
  **speed**: a steeper line means faster, and a flat line means the object is at rest.
- A **velocity–time graph** shows the velocity at each moment. Its **slope** is the **acceleration**, and the
  **area** under it is the **displacement**.

A graph is not a picture of the road. A line rising up the page doesn't mean the object is going uphill.

To find a slope, pick two points on a straight line: slope = change in the vertical value ÷ change in time.
