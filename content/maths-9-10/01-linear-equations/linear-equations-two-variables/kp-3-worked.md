## Worked example: the graph and its intercepts

Draw the graph of $2x + 3y = 12$.

1. **x-axis crossing**: every point on the x-axis has $y = 0$. Put $y = 0$: $2x = 12$, so $x = 6$. Point $(6, 0)$.
2. **y-axis crossing**: every point on the y-axis has $x = 0$. Put $x = 0$: $3y = 12$, so $y = 4$. Point $(0, 4)$.
3. A third point as a check: $x = 3$ gives $3y = 6$, so $y = 2$. Point $(3, 2)$.
4. Plot the three points; they lie on one straight line. Draw it through them.
