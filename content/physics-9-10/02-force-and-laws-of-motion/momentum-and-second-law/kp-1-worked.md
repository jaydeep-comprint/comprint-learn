## Worked example: momentum, p = mv

**A ball and a truck.** A cricket ball of mass 160 g is bowled at 25 m/s. A truck of mass 4000 kg rolls
slowly at 2 m/s. Which has more momentum?

First convert the ball's mass to kilograms: 160 g = 160 ÷ 1000 = 0.16 kg.

- Ball: p = 0.16 × 25 = **4 kg m/s**
- Truck: p = 4000 × 2 = **8000 kg m/s**

The truck has far more momentum, even though it is slow, because its mass is so large. If the truck were
parked (v = 0), its momentum would be zero.

**Converting km/h.** A car of mass 1000 kg moves at 72 km/h. To get m/s, *divide* by 3.6:
72 ÷ 3.6 = 20 m/s. Then p = 1000 × 20 = **20 000 kg m/s**.

**Direction.** Momentum points the same way as the velocity. Take east as positive: a 50 kg runner moving
west at 4 m/s has velocity −4 m/s, so her momentum is 50 × (−4) = **−200 kg m/s**.

**Working backwards.** If a 5 kg trolley has a momentum of 15 kg m/s, its velocity is v = p ÷ m = 15 ÷ 5 = 3 m/s.
