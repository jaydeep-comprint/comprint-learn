# Forces and inertia

A **force** is a push or a pull. It can start an object moving, stop it, speed it up, slow it down, turn it or
change its shape. Forces are measured in **newtons (N)**, and every force has a direction.

- When the forces on an object cancel, they are **balanced**: the **net force** is zero and the motion does not change.
- When they don't cancel, there is an **unbalanced** (net) force: the object speeds up, slows down or turns.

**Newton's first law**: an object stays at rest, or keeps moving in a straight line at a constant speed, unless an
unbalanced force acts on it. Moving things on Earth slow down because **friction** acts against their motion,
not because their "force runs out".

This tendency to keep doing what it is already doing is called **inertia**. The **mass** of an object is the
measure of its inertia: the more mass, the harder it is to start, stop or turn.
