# Speed and velocity

**Speed** tells you how fast something moves: the distance covered per unit time.
**Velocity** tells you how fast *and in which direction*: the displacement per unit time.

- Average speed = total distance ÷ total time
- Average velocity = displacement ÷ time

Both are measured in metres per second (m/s) or kilometres per hour (km/h). Because
1 km/h = 1000 m ÷ 3600 s, **1 m/s = 3.6 km/h**: divide by 3.6 to go from km/h to m/s.

A car going round a bend at a steady 40 km/h has constant speed, but its velocity changes because its
direction changes.
