# Pair of linear equations: algebraic methods

Graphs show *how many* solutions a pair has, but reading the exact point is hard. Algebra finds it exactly.

- **Substitution**: make one variable the subject of one equation, then substitute that *whole expression* into the other equation. You get one equation in one variable.
- **Elimination**: multiply the equations so one variable has the same (or opposite) coefficient, then subtract (or add) to remove it.

Either way, finish by substituting back to find the second variable, and **check the pair in both original equations**.
