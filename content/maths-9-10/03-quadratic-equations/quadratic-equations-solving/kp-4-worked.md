## Worked example: word problems

A train travels 360 km at a uniform speed. If the speed had been 5 km/h more, the journey would have taken
1 hour less. Find the speed of the train.

1. **Choose the unknown**: let the speed be $x$ km/h. Time $= \dfrac{\text{distance}}{\text{speed}}$, so the
   time is $\dfrac{360}{x}$ hours, and at the faster speed $\dfrac{360}{x + 5}$ hours.
2. **Form the equation**: the faster journey takes 1 hour less, so $\dfrac{360}{x} - \dfrac{360}{x + 5} = 1$.
3. **Make it quadratic**: multiply both sides by $x(x + 5)$: $360(x + 5) - 360x = x(x + 5)$, so
   $1800 = x^2 + 5x$, that is $x^2 + 5x - 1800 = 0$.
4. **Solve**: two numbers with product $-1800$ and sum $5$ are $45$ and $-40$:
   $(x + 45)(x - 40) = 0$, so $x = 40$ or $x = -45$.
5. **Check against the situation**: a speed can't be negative, so reject $-45$. The speed is **40 km/h**.
   Check: $\dfrac{360}{40} = 9$ h and $\dfrac{360}{45} = 8$ h, 1 hour less. ✓
