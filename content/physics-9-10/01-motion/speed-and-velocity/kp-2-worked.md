## Worked example: velocity and unit conversion

A car moves from x = −20 m to x = 60 m (east positive) in 8 s.

- Displacement = 60 − (−20) = 80 m east
- Average velocity = 80 m ÷ 8 s = **10 m/s east**

Converting: 10 m/s × 3.6 = **36 km/h**. Going the other way, 72 km/h ÷ 3.6 = 20 m/s.
(A quick check: m/s numbers are always *smaller* than the same speed in km/h.)
