## Worked example: substitution

Solve $x + y = 14$ and $x - 4y = 4$.

1. From the first equation: $y = 14 - x$.
2. Substitute the whole expression into the second: $x - 4(14 - x) = 4$.
3. Remove the bracket carefully: $x - 56 + 4x = 4$, so $5x = 60$ and $x = 12$.
4. Back-substitute: $y = 14 - 12 = 2$.
5. Check: $12 + 2 = 14$ ✓ and $12 - 4(2) = 4$ ✓.
