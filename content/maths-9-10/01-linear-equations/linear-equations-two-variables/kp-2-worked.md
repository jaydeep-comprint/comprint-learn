## Worked example: finding solutions

Find the value of $y$ when $x = 2$ for $4x + 3y = 23$, then write $y$ in terms of $x$.

1. Substitute $x = 2$: $4(2) + 3y = 23$, so $8 + 3y = 23$.
2. Subtract 8 from both sides: $3y = 15$, so $y = 5$. The pair $(2, 5)$ is a solution.
3. In general: $3y = 23 - 4x$, so $y = \dfrac{23 - 4x}{3}$. Divide **every** term on the right by 3.

Any value of $x$ gives a matching $y$, which is why there are infinitely many solutions.
