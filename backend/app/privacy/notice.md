## About the AI tutor

Your child's school uses a learning app run by [OPERATOR NAME] to practise maths, physics and Python.
The app has an **AI tutor**. When your child is stuck on a question, they can ask it for a hint or
type a short question to it. The tutor points them back to the worked example, to a topic they need
first, or to a small experiment to try. **It never gives the answer or the next step.**

**What is sent to the AI service.** When your child uses the tutor, the app sends the question they
are working on, their earlier tries at it and what they type to the tutor. It does **not** send their
name, username, school or any other details about them.

**Where it goes.** The tutor uses DeepSeek, an AI service whose servers are in **China**.

**How long it is kept.** The app keeps the text of tutor chats for **180 days** and then deletes it.
If you withdraw your consent, your child's chat text is deleted **at once**.

**Consent is optional.** Without it, your child can still use everything else in the app: lessons,
practice, reviews and quizzes. Only the AI tutor stays locked.

**Changing your mind.** You can withdraw consent at any time by telling your child's class teacher.

**Your child's data.** To ask for a copy of the data the app stores about your child, or to have it
erased, contact the school. Questions about the app: [CONTACT].

## What this app stores about you

- **Your account**: your display name and username. Students have no email address in the app.
- **Your learning**: your answers, how long you spent, the hints you used, and which topics you have
  learned, so the app can choose your next practice and reviews.
- **Your progress**: XP, streaks, goals, quiz and placement results.
- **AI tutor chats** (only with consent): the text is deleted after 180 days, or at once if consent
  is withdrawn.
- **Teacher summaries**: your teachers can ask an AI for a summary of the class's progress. It
  receives numbers about the class with students replaced by codes like "S3", never names.

Your teachers and your school's admins can see your learning progress. Other students can only see
you on a class leaderboard if your teacher turns it on.
