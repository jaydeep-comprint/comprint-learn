## Worked example: from words to a pair of equations

2 pens and 3 notebooks cost ₹110; 4 pens and 1 notebook cost ₹70. Find the price of each.

1. Let a pen cost ₹$x$ and a notebook ₹$y$.
2. Translate: $2x + 3y = 110$ and $4x + y = 70$.
3. From the second: $y = 70 - 4x$. Substitute: $2x + 3(70 - 4x) = 110$, so $210 - 10x = 110$ and $x = 10$.
4. Then $y = 70 - 40 = 30$. A pen costs ₹10 and a notebook ₹30.
5. Check against the words: $2(10) + 3(30) = 110$ ✓, $4(10) + 30 = 70$ ✓.
