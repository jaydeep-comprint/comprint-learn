## Worked example: lines, axes and areas

The line $3x + 2y = 12$ meets the x-axis at $A$ and the y-axis at $B$. Find the area of triangle $OAB$.

1. $A$: put $y = 0$, so $3x = 12$ and $A = (4, 0)$.
2. $B$: put $x = 0$, so $2y = 12$ and $B = (0, 6)$.
3. $OAB$ is a right triangle with legs $OA = 4$ and $OB = 6$.
4. Area $= \frac{1}{2} \times 4 \times 6 = 12$ square units.
