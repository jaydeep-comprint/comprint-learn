## Worked example: there and back along a road

Asha walks 60 m east along a straight road, then 20 m west.

- **Distance** = 60 m + 20 m = **80 m** (add every part of the path).
- **Displacement** = 60 m east − 20 m west = **40 m east** (where she ends up compared with the start).

If she had walked back the full 60 m, her displacement would be **0 m** — but she would still have
covered 120 m of distance.
