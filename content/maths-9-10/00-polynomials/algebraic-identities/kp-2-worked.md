## Worked example: (x + a)(x + b)

$(x + a)(x + b) = x^2 + bx + ax + ab = x^2 + (a + b)x + ab$.
The middle coefficient is the **sum** of the two numbers, and the constant term is their **product**.

**a)** $(x + 4)(x + 6) = x^2 + (4 + 6)x + 4 \times 6 = x^2 + 10x + 24$.

**b)** $(x + 7)(x - 3)$: here $a = 7$ and $b = -3$, so $a + b = 4$ and $ab = -21$:
$x^2 + 4x - 21$.

**c)** $(y - 2)(y - 5)$: $a = -2$, $b = -5$, so $a + b = -7$ and $ab = +10$: $y^2 - 7y + 10$.
Two negative numbers multiply to a positive one.

**d)** $104 \times 105 = (100 + 4)(100 + 5) = 10000 + 9 \times 100 + 20 = 10920$.
