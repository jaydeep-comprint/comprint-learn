# Newton's third law of motion

Forces always come in pairs. When one body pushes or pulls another, the second body pushes or pulls back on
the first.

**Third law of motion**: to every action there is an equal and opposite reaction.

The two forces of an action–reaction pair:

- are **equal in size** and **opposite in direction**,
- are the **same kind** of force (a push and a push, a pull of gravity and a pull of gravity),
- act **at the same time**, and
- act on **two different bodies**.

When you walk, your foot pushes the ground backward, and the ground pushes your foot forward. That forward
push is what moves you.

Because the two forces act on different bodies, they never cancel each other. Each body moves according to the
force on *it*: a = F ÷ m. The same force gives a light body a big acceleration and a heavy body a small one.
