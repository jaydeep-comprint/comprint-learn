## Worked example: adding, subtracting and multiplying

Let $p(x) = 2x^2 + 3x - 1$ and $q(x) = x^2 - 5x + 4$.

**Add**: collect like terms (same power of $x$): $p(x) + q(x) = 3x^2 - 2x + 3$.
Only the coefficients add: $2x^2 + x^2 = 3x^2$, never $3x^4$.

**Subtract**: the minus sign changes **every** sign in the second bracket:
$p(x) - q(x) = 2x^2 + 3x - 1 - x^2 + 5x - 4 = x^2 + 8x - 5$.

**Multiply**: multiply each term of the first by each term of the second, adding the powers:
$(2x + 3)(x - 4) = 2x^2 - 8x + 3x - 12 = 2x^2 - 5x - 12$.

The degree of a product is the sum of the degrees: here $1 + 1 = 2$.
